Nokutenda

/no·ku·te·nda/from kutenda

  1. With thanks.
  2. With faith; by believing.

Your name means with thanks.

Which is exactly how I feel about you. Every day I get to spend with you, I'm thankful.

Kutenda also means to believe. So your name carries both: gratitude, and faith. I'm thankful for you, and I believe in you. Today, and every day after it.

    Nokutenda,

    Happy eighteenth birthday. As of today you're officially a grown-up, though if I'm honest you've been wise beyond your years for as long as I've known you.

    Eighteen years ago, on a Tuesday under a new moon, the world got you. Since then you've travelled almost seventeen billion kilometres around the Sun, and somewhere along the way you found me. Your name says exactly how I feel about that: nokutenda, with thanks.

    You are kind in a way that makes people feel safe. You're beautiful in ways you don't even notice, and stronger than you give yourself credit for. I hope eighteen is the year you finally see yourself the way I see you.

    Whatever this next chapter brings, I'll be right there cheering you on.
