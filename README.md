# For Nokutenda

A birthday site for Nokutenda's 18th (28 October 2008 → 28 October 2026), from Bete.

A single self-contained page (`index.html`): envelope opening, a tape that rewinds eighteen years to the night she was born and fast-forwards back, the night she was born (new moon, Scorpio, opal, marigold), live counters of her trips around the Sun, 18 wishes hidden in the stars, a cake with 18 candles you can blow out through the microphone, and a letter.

Before 28 October the envelope stays sealed behind a countdown and opens on its own at midnight. Opened later, it first rewinds day by day back to the 28th. If she saw the countdown earlier, the opening starts with "You came back."

- `#me` once on your own phone: skips the countdown and leaves your visits out of the analytics.
- `#countdown`: preview the sealed countdown screen.
- `#skip`: jump past the opening.
