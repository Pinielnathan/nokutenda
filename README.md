# For Nokutenda

A birthday site for Nokutenda's 18th (28 October 2008 → 28 October 2026), from Bete.

A single self-contained page (`index.html`): envelope opening, the date landing on 28 October, the night she was born (new moon, Scorpio, opal, marigold), live counters of her trips around the Sun, 18 wishes hidden in the stars, a cake with 18 candles you can blow out through the microphone, and a letter.

Add `#skip` to the URL to jump past the opening, or `#me` once on your own phone so your visits are left out of the analytics.
